# 🏏 Barakah Blasters — Official Cricket Website

> **"Blessed with Grace. Blasting with Glory."**

A modern, high-energy, creative website for the **Barakah Blasters** cricket franchise. Built with focused simplicity, a crisp **Light Athletic Theme** (Pearl White, Deep Royal Emerald & Sunburst Gold), and an interactive experience showcasing the team's elite **10-player squad**.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Creative Features

- **Grand 10-Player Championship Banner (Interactive & 4K Downloadable)**:
  - Spectacular full-width panorama featuring all 10 players side-by-side with colossal **"BARAKAH BLASTERS"** background typography (dual fill & neon stroke layers).
  - Ambient stadium spotlight beams, dynamic hover elevation with gold searchlights, and sibling dimming for focus.
  - Interactive tooltip revealing each player's signature weapon on hover; 1-click smooth-scroll with golden pulse animation to the player's profile card in the Squad Hub.
  - 1-click download button for an ultra-high-resolution **4K PNG banner graphic** (`assets/barakah-blasters-squad-banner.png`, 2400x1050), custom-generated with official crest, player studio portraits, jersey numbers `#1` to `#10`, roles, and Captain/VC insignia.
- **Official H.P.L - 7 Logo & Branding**:
  - Integrated the official **Barakah Blasters H.P.L - 7** shield crest with transparent cutout across the Navbar, Hero emblem card, Match Center, and Footer.
  - Kit theme aligned with the official Midnight Navy & Athletic Gold tournament jersey.
- **Hero 10-Player Creative Sliding Showcase**:
  - Dynamic, auto-sliding stage right in the hero banner cycling through all 10 players slide-by-slide every 3.2s with pause on hover.
  - Interactive navigation: previous/next chevron buttons, 10 progress pips (#1 to #10), slide counter (`01 / 10`), and touch swipe support.
  - Shows player portrait photo, frosted jersey badge (#1..#10), Captain/VC badge, role pill, and signature weapon chip.
- **Official 10-Player Squad Hub**:
  - Multi-view controls: **4-in-a-line Card Grid**, horizontal **Slide Reel Carousel**, and **Interactive Tactical Pitch**.
  - High-definition studio portrait photos for all 10 players wearing the official H.P.L - 7 cap & jersey.
- **Tactical Pitch View (Creative 2D Field)**:
  - Interactive cricket ground with fielding pins for all 10 players in their real-world fielding spots.
  - Click any position on the pitch to spotlight that player's role, signature delivery, and stats.
- **Synthesized Cricket Sound Effects (Web Audio API)**:
  - Procedural crisp willow cricket bat strike ("THWACK!") & stadium cheer effects synthesized on-the-fly without external audio file dependencies.
  - Persistent sound toggle (Mute / Unmute).
- **Match Center & Live Countdown**:
  - Live countdown timer to **Match 02** opening clash (Wednesday, 21 October 2026, 7:00 AM at **Trimohoni Cricket Ground**).
  - Official **H.P.L - 7** Barakah Blasters tournament schedule (Matches 02, 03, 05, 06, and the Grand Final).
- **The Creed (Barakah DNA)**:
  - The 3 Pillars: *Barakah (Blessed Intent)*, *Blasting Firepower*, and *Ten Titans, One Soul*.
  - Team facts (Ground & Fortress: **Trimohoni Cricket Ground**, Head Coach, Team Colors).
- **Zero-Dependency & Instant Run**:
  - Open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or run the local Node server.

## 🚀 How to Run

### Option 1: One-Click Launch (Windows)
Double-click `start-website.bat` in the project root folder. It will launch the local server and open the website in your default browser.

### Option 2: Run via Node
```bash
node server.js
```
Open **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)** in your browser.

### Option 3: Direct Browser Open
Simply double-click `index.html` to open it directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Firefox.
