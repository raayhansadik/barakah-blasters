Franchise Identity

The Blaster Creed

More than cricket. Barakah Blasters is built upon enduring principles that guide every innings, run chase, and bowling spell.

1. Barakah (Blessed Intent)

We play with absolute integrity, gratitude, and moral sportsmanship. True victory begins inside before the first ball is bowled.

2. Blasting Firepower

Fearless aggressive modern cricket. We take the game to the opposition with electric running, searing pace, and towering sixes.

3. Ten Titans, One Soul

Every warrior defends for the other. When one blaster strikes, all ten celebrate with identical passion and shared brotherhood.

4. Ice In The Veins

Surgical execution under maximum stadium pressure. When the match hangs on 6 deliveries, we stay ice-cold and deliver perfection.
